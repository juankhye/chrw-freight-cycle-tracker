The spread

What shippers pay vs what truckers cost

DAT dry van national averages. Spot above contract squeezes Robinson's 70% contract book; spot rolling back below contract after a bid season is the broker's sweet spot. The truckload producer price index from the BLS is the free, long-history proxy for contract pricing.

DAT dry van spot vs contract, $/mile

Spot minus contract spread, $/mile, and tender rejections

Spread below zero after a bid reset is the 2022 pattern that produced record margins. Tender rejection index (SONAR OTRI, %) rising means carriers refuse contract loads to chase spot: an early warning of squeeze. Manual series from news coverage; exact daily values require a SONAR licence.

Truckload PPI (BLS, long-distance TL), y/y %

FRED PCU484121484121, monthly, auto-refreshed. Blends contract and spot, includes fuel. Peaks in this series (2018, 2022) preceded Robinson's earnings peaks by 6 to 12 months; troughs (2019, 2023) preceded the share-price bottoms.

Diesel, $/gallon (EIA weekly) and y/y

FRED GASDESW, auto-refreshed. Fuel is a pass-through surcharge indexed to this series, so it inflates gross revenue and dilutes margin percentage without changing gross profit dollars. It also raises small-carrier costs, which pushes capacity out.
Demand

Is anyone actually shipping more?

The 2026 rally is supply-driven. The bull case needs the demand leg to arrive while capacity is still capped. Cass Freight Index, auto-refreshed from FRED.

Cass shipments y/y % vs Robinson NAST truckload volume y/y %

Robinson beating Cass is the share-gain evidence (13 straight quarters through 2Q26). Cass shipments negative for 15 quarters is the demand trough. Watch for the Cass line crossing zero.

Cass expenditures y/y and implied cost per shipment y/y

Expenditures ÷ shipments is a free proxy for the all-in freight rate. When it runs far ahead of shipments, rates are doing the work, not volume: exactly the current picture.
Capacity

Trucks and drivers: leaving now, returning when?

Capacity leaving pushes spot up (squeeze now). Capacity returning caps spot (broker sweet spot later). Orders lead deliveries by 6 to 18 months.

Class 8 net orders, units per month

Manual series from ACT Research / FTR preliminary releases. Replacement demand is roughly 18k to 20k a month; sustained orders above 30k signal a capacity flood 12 to 18 months out.

FMCSA net change in for-hire carrier authorities

Manual series from FTR / Carrier Details as reported in trade press. Negative = more carriers exiting than entering = supply leaving. This is the series behind the 2026 "supply-driven recovery".

Truck transportation employment (BLS), y/y %

FRED CES4348400001, auto-refreshed. A slow but public read on driver supply. Falling employment with rising rates is the 2026 signature.

Enforcement and exit events

Drivers placed out of service on English-proficiency checks, non-domiciled CDL revocations, notable carrier failures. Entered by hand as reported.
Robinson's own disclosures

The gap, measured at the company

Robinson discloses truckload price per mile and cost per mile changes every quarter. The difference is the squeeze; the NAST gross margin is its result. The company also gives three monthly AGP-per-day readings on each call, the only intra-quarter signal it provides.

Truckload price per mile vs cost per mile, y/y % (18 years)

Source: Goldman Sachs model "Volume, Pricing, Cost History" tab (company-disclosed, 1Q08 to 1Q26) plus quarterly releases since. Cost above price = squeeze (2011, 2014, 2018, 2021, 2026). Price above cost = sweet spot (2009, 2016, 2019, 2022-23).

NAST gross margin % and adjusted operating margin %

Gross margin = AGP ÷ gross revenue (the spread). Operating margin = operating income ÷ AGP (the cost base). The bull case is the first line recovering to 14%+ while the second holds near 40%.

AGP per business day, y/y % by month

Management's monthly cadence, given on each earnings call. Deceleration inside a quarter (Apr +9, May +7, Jun +3 in 2Q26) is what the bears cited.

Cycle analogs

How the last two squeezes played out, aligned at the start

Quarter 0 is the first quarter in which Robinson's truckload cost per mile rose faster than its price per mile by more than 3 points. Lines show what happened over the following two years. If 2026 follows the pattern, the margin trough is one to two quarters away and the share price bottoms before it.

Price minus cost gap, percentage points, from squeeze start

Positive = spread widening. 2017-18 and 2020-21 both spent about four quarters negative before turning.

Share price, indexed to 100 at squeeze start

In both prior squeezes the stock was flat to up during the squeeze and ran in the catch-up phase. 2026 is the first squeeze where the stock fell, and the difference is the July verdict.
